# Figure Scorecard

## Scoring rubric

| Category | Points | Full-credit test |
|---|---:|---|
| Information | 20 | The figure answers one explicit question and the primary message is immediately identifiable. |
| Scientific Accuracy | 20 | Scales, transformations, uncertainty, outliers, and visual encodings are honest and disclosed. |
| Readability | 15 | Text is legible at final size; axes, units, ticks, and legend are unambiguous. |
| Visual Hierarchy | 15 | Primary evidence dominates; context is subordinate; highlights are rare and meaningful. |
| Color | 10 | Semantic roles are correct, restrained, color-blind aware, and grayscale-resilient. |
| Layout | 10 | Plot area, white space, alignment, and aspect ratio support reading. |
| Style Consistency | 10 | Typography, axes, spacing, and semantic encodings match this system. |
| **TOTAL** | **100** |  |

## Status thresholds

- 0–84: `DRAFT — REVISION REQUIRED`; never FINAL.
- 85–91: `ACCEPTABLE`.
- 92–100: `CORE FIGURE QUALITY`.
